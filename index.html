About Vatayanam

European engineering, Bharat make.

V-Tech Building Systems Pvt. Ltd was established in 2012 and works with a team of over 150 professionals. Its manufacturing facility can produce up to 100 windows per day under stringent quality standards. V-Tech has partnered with Fundermax for HPL cladding and Wesmarc for premium rebated engineered wood doors.

Backed by its experience in facade and fenestration, V-Tech created VATAYANAM, a dedicated facility and team for System Aluminium windows and doors. Its products combine sound insulation, wind-load strength, water tightness, air permeability and impact resistance with sleek, modern design.

Visit the Experience Centre at No. 693, M.N.S. Arcade, Dr. Rajkumar Road, 6th Block, Rajajinagar, Bengaluru 560 010. Works: Kalenahalli, Shivakote, Hesaraghatta, Bengaluru 560 089.

Questions

Good to know.

Which series suits very large openings?

Pearl and Emerald go up to a window height of 3660mm, and the Vivid series is made for very large spaces with door heights up to 4600mm. Final suitability should be confirmed with V-Tech’s technical department.

Can the sliding windows take an insect screen?

The Opal, Sapphire and Pearl series list a provision for an insect screen in the brochure.

What glass thickness is supported?

It depends on the series: Opal 5 to 18mm, Sapphire and Pearl 5 to 24mm, Quartz 5 to 38mm. Vivid takes 8 to 26mm glass.

Are the windows burglar-proof?

The brochure lists Opal, Sapphire and Pearl as bug and burglar proof windows, and Topaz, Quartz and the glazing systems as burglar-proof window and door systems.

Which series is made for balconies?

The Jett series is designed specifically for balconies, with continuous railing top and bottom sections that lift the building elevation.

Can I get tilt & turn or slide & fold?

Yes. The Quartz 50mm casement series is tilt & turn compatible and offers sliding folding doors.

Where can I see the products?

Visit the V-Tech Experience Centre at No. 693, M.N.S. Arcade, Dr. Rajkumar Road, 6th Block, Rajajinagar, Bengaluru 560 010.

Are the technical values final?

No. The brochure states that its data is for reference and advises taking prior confirmation from the technical department.
